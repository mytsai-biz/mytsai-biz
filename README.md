## How I Think

Across my work, I tend to focus on five questions:

**1. What is the strategic thesis?**  
What must be true for the transaction, partnership, or initiative to create value?

**2. What has to change operationally?**  
Translate the thesis into concrete decisions, owners, dependencies, and milestones.

**3. Where can value leak?**  
Identify the failure points across talent, customers, technology, economics, governance, and execution.

**4. How do we measure progress?**  
Build operating rhythms and KPIs that allow executives to see whether the thesis is actually being realized.

**5. How does the first success become repeatable?**  
Turn individual wins into playbooks, systems, partnerships, and capabilities that compound.
